Privacy Policy

Version 1.5 · September 2026 · Judo Academy

This Privacy Policy explains what data Judo Academy collects, how it is used and how it is protected. Judo Academy is designed to be used by learners of all ages, including children, and this policy has been written to comply with the Google Play Families Policy, COPPA (US) and the GDPR, including its provisions on children's data (GDPR-K). Our principle is simple: we collect only what is necessary for the application to function.

1. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The following data is stored on your device. Items marked with an asterisk (*) are also securely synced to our servers if you sign in with a Google account, so your progress stays available across devices:

  • XP, level, badges and training statistics *
  • Judo Journal entries and notes *
  • AI Sensei conversation history (up to 100 messages) *
  • Application settings (language, theme, sound, haptics)
  • Streak and daily progress
  • Belt profile and grading date *

Clearing application data or uninstalling the application permanently deletes all local data. Synced data can be deleted at any time as described in Section 2.2.

2. Data Transmitted to the Server (Optional)

The following data is transmitted to our servers only if you sign in with a Google account:

  • Email address and Google ID (account identification)
  • Username / first and last name (as chosen or provided)
  • Profile photo (as provided by your Google account, if available)
  • Club name and country (if you choose to add them to your profile)
  • Belt and grading date (used to sync your progress across devices)
  • Birth year (used to personalise AI recommendations and to determine whether age-appropriate safeguards, such as a parental confirmation step, apply to your account — see Section 5)
  • Total XP (for leaderboard display)
  • Judo Journal entries, notes and AI Sensei conversation history (used to keep this data available across devices)
  • Purchase and subscription status (Premium), managed through Google Play Billing
  • A screenshot you choose to attach to a bug report, if you submit one

Without Google sign-in, no data is transmitted to the server. The application is fully functional without signing in.

2.1 Server Location

Server data is stored on servers located in the European Union. Data is not transferred outside the EU, except where described in Section 3.3 (third-party services) for the specific, limited purposes listed there.

2.2 Data Retention and Deletion

Data is retained for as long as you use the application. You, or — for a child's account — a parent or guardian, may request deletion of all data, or of a specific part of it (for example only the AI Sensei conversation history), without needing to delete the whole account, at any time:

  • Using the in-app account deletion option, or
  • Via our Account & Data Deletion page, or
  • By emailing judo.academy.world@gmail.com

Requests are processed within 30 days. Data stored only locally on your device (see Section 1) is not affected by a server-side deletion request and is removed by uninstalling the application or clearing its data.

3. AI Features

The application uses a cloud AI service for: AI Sensei (personalised judo advisor), Scouting (opponent analysis) and Judo Journal (training analysis). The AI provider is Anthropic (Claude models).

3.1 What Is Sent to the AI Service

  • The text of your message or entry
  • Belt and level context (if you have set up a profile)
  • Previous messages within the same session

3.2 What Is NOT Sent

  • Your name, email or identifying information
  • History from previous sessions
  • Any local data other than what you explicitly enter

Anthropic processes this data as our data processor, in accordance with its own privacy policy, solely to generate a response to your request. Data sent to the AI service is not permanently stored on our servers beyond what is described in Section 1. If our AI provider changes, this policy and the in-app settings will be updated accordingly.

3.3 Other Third-Party Services

Besides our AI provider, we use a small number of other services to operate the application. Each only receives the specific data needed for its purpose:

  • Google (Sign-In / OAuth): used to authenticate your account. Google receives the basic profile information involved in sign-in (email, name, profile photo).
  • Google Play Billing: processes Premium purchases and subscriptions. We do not receive or store your payment card details — only the resulting purchase/subscription status.
  • Cloudinary: stores screenshots that you voluntarily attach to a bug report, so we can review and fix the issue. Used only if you choose to attach a screenshot.

We do not use any advertising SDKs, and we do not share data with data brokers or use it for behavioural advertising.

4. Analytics and Tracking

The application does not use third-party analytics or crash-reporting tools (Google Analytics, Firebase, Crashlytics, Mixpanel or similar). Basic app-interaction events and error diagnostics are recorded only in our own database, for the sole purpose of improving the application — they are never sold, shared with advertisers, or used for behavioural advertising. We do not use cookies. The only server logs are standard access logs (IP address, request time, endpoint), automatically deleted after 7 days.

5. Children and Young Users

Judo Academy is designed to be usable by young learners, including children under 13, alongside teenagers and adults. Registration requires a minimum age of 8. We are committed to following the Google Play Families Policy, COPPA and GDPR-K.

Because of this, the application applies the following safeguards for accounts identified as belonging to a child, based on the birth year provided at sign-up:

  • Parental confirmation before any AI feature: the first time a child account uses AI Sensei, Scouting or Judo Journal analysis, a simple confirmation step is shown asking a parent or guardian to acknowledge the feature before it becomes available. This confirmation is shared across all three AI features and is remembered for that account going forward.
  • Parental confirmation before every purchase: a child account is shown a confirmation step before any Premium purchase is completed, to prevent accidental or unsupervised spending. This step is shown every time, and is not remembered.
  • No behavioural advertising, no ads: the application contains no advertising SDKs of any kind.
  • No third-party analytics targeting children: as described in Section 4, all analytics stay on our own servers and are used only to operate and improve the application.
  • Minimal data collection: we only collect what is described in Sections 1–2, and never collect precise location, contacts, or other data unrelated to the application's core function.

A parent or guardian may review, correct, or request deletion of a child's data at any time using the methods described in Section 2.2. If you believe a child has provided us with more personal data than is described in this policy, please contact us at judo.academy.world@gmail.com so that we can review and, where appropriate, delete it promptly.

6. Your Rights (GDPR)

If you use the application from the European Union, you (or, for a child's account, a parent or guardian acting on the child's behalf) have the following rights:

  • Right of access: You may request access to all data we hold about you.
  • Right to erasure: You may request deletion of all, or part, of your data.
  • Right to rectification: You may request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Right to portability: You may request a copy of your data in a readable format.
  • Right to object: You may object to the processing of your data.

To exercise your rights, write to judo.academy.world@gmail.com. We respond within 30 days.

7. Data Security

All communication between the application and the server is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). Server access is restricted and protected by authentication. Passwords are not stored — we use Google OAuth exclusively, together with a signed session token to keep you securely signed in.

8. Changes to This Policy

You will be notified of significant changes via an in-app notification at least 14 days in advance. The date of the last update is always visible in the header of this document.

9. Contact and Complaints

  • Email: judo.academy.world@gmail.com
  • Response time: Within 30 days of receiving your request
  • Application: Judo Academy (com.judoacademy.app)
  • Date: September 2026

If you believe your data has not been handled correctly, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the data protection authority in your country.
