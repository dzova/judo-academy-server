Privacy Policy

Version 1.3 · September 2026 · Judo Academy

This Privacy Policy explains what data Judo Academy collects, how it is used and how it is protected. Our principle is simple: we collect only what is necessary for the application to function.

1. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The following data is stored on your device. Items marked with an asterisk (*) are also securely synced to our servers if you sign in with a Google account, so your progress stays available across devices:

  • XP, level, badges and training statistics *
  • Judo Journal entries and notes *
  • AI Sensei conversation history (up to 100 messages) *
  • Application settings (language, theme, sound, haptics)
  • Streak and daily progress
  • Belt profile and grading date *

Clearing application data or uninstalling the application permanently deletes all local data. Synced data can be deleted at any time as described in Section 2.2.

2. Data Transmitted to the Server (Optional)

The following data is transmitted to our servers only if you sign in with a Google account:

  • Email address and Google ID (account identification)
  • Username (nickname you have chosen)
  • Profile photo (as provided by your Google account, if available)
  • Club name and country (if you choose to add them to your profile)
  • Belt and grading date (used to sync your progress across devices)
  • Birth year and dominant hand/side (used to personalise AI recommendations, if you choose to add them to your profile)
  • Total XP (for leaderboard display)
  • Judo Journal entries, notes and AI Sensei conversation history (used to keep this data available across devices)

Without Google sign-in, no data is transmitted to the server. The application is fully functional without signing in.

2.1 Server Location

Server data is stored on servers located in the European Union. Data is not transferred outside the EU.

2.2 Data Retention

Data is retained for as long as you use the application. You may request deletion of all your data by contacting us at judo.academy.world@gmail.com. Deletion is carried out within 30 days.

3. AI Features

The application uses cloud AI services for: AI Sensei (personalised judo advisor), Scouting (opponent analysis) and Judo Journal (training analysis).

3.1 What Is Sent to the AI Service

  • The text of your message or entry
  • Belt and level context (if you have set up a profile)
  • Previous messages within the same session

3.2 What Is NOT Sent

  • Your name, email or identifying information
  • History from previous sessions
  • Any local data other than what you explicitly enter

The AI service processes data in accordance with its own privacy policy. Data sent to the AI service is not permanently stored on our servers. The current AI provider is listed in the application settings; the provider may change, and users will be notified of any such change.

4. Analytics and Tracking

The application does not use third-party analytics tools (Google Analytics, Firebase, Mixpanel or similar). We do not use cookies. The only server logs are standard access logs (IP address, request time, endpoint) which are automatically deleted after 7 days.

5. Children and Young Users

The application is intended for users aged 13 and above. It is not directed at, and is not intended to be used by, children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 13.

Parental supervision is recommended for users aged 13-15 when using AI-powered features (AI Sensei, Scouting, Judo Journal).

If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal data, please contact us at judo.academy.world@gmail.com so that we can delete it within 48 hours.

6. Your Rights (GDPR)

If you use the application from the European Union, you have the following rights:

  • Right of access: You may request access to all data we hold about you.
  • Right to erasure: You may request deletion of all your data.
  • Right to rectification: You may request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Right to portability: You may request a copy of your data in a readable format.
  • Right to object: You may object to the processing of your data.

To exercise your rights, write to judo.academy.world@gmail.com. We respond within 30 days.

7. Data Security

All communication between the application and the server is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). Server access is restricted and protected by authentication. Passwords are not stored — we use Google OAuth exclusively, together with a signed session token to keep you securely signed in.

8. Changes to This Policy

You will be notified of significant changes via an in-app notification at least 14 days in advance. The date of the last update is always visible in the header of this document.

9. Contact and Complaints

  • Publisher: Judo Academy
  • Email: judo.academy.world@gmail.com
  • Response time: Within 30 days of receiving your request
  • Application: Judo Academy (com.judoacademy.app)
  • Date: September 2026

If you believe your data has not been handled correctly, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the data protection authority in your country.
