Judo Academy

An educational mobile app for learning judo techniques, terminology and rules through interactive quizzes.

What is Judo Academy?

Judo Academy is an Android application designed for judo practitioners of all ages — especially children and youth training at judo clubs. The app helps users learn and review judo throws, grappling techniques, Japanese terminology, and competition rules through gamified quizzes, a technique encyclopedia, and progress tracking tied to belt ranks (kyu/dan system).

Key features

  • Quiz-based learning of judo techniques, terms, and rules
  • Technique library organized by belt level
  • Progress tracking and achievements
  • Optional account sign-in (via Google) to sync progress across devices

Why does Judo Academy use Google Sign-In?

Signing in with Google is optional and is used only to save a user's quiz progress, belt rank, and achievements to their account, so this progress can be restored if they reinstall the app or switch to a new phone. Judo Academy only requests a user's basic Google profile information (name, email address, and profile photo) to create and identify their account — it does not request access to Gmail, Google Drive, contacts, or any other Google data. Users who prefer not to sign in can still use the app's core features (quizzes, technique library) without a Google account, though their progress will then be stored locally on the device only.

Who is behind this app?

Judo Academy is developed independently in cooperation with local judo clubs, as a learning tool for their students. For questions, support, or feedback, contact us at judo.academy.world@gmail.com.
